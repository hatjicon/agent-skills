# Strategic Document Builder

An Agent Skill for building **evidence-based product strategy documents** that connect customer problems, strategic direction, business impact, roadmap decisions, and execution.

## What it does
- Develops an executive-ready strategic narrative, starting with the problem and why it matters now.
- Separates customer problems, evidence, strategic direction, and solution concepts.
- Structures competitive analysis, business value, success criteria, and execution dependencies.
- Makes uncertainty explicit with **NEEDS CLARIFICATION**, **DISCOVERY NEEDED**, and **LOW CONFIDENCE** markers.
- Provides reusable templates for full strategy documents and individual strategic bets.

## Installation
Copy the `strategic-document-builder` folder, including `SKILL.md`, into the skills directory supported by your AI agent. Consult your agent's documentation for its specific installation path.

## Example prompt
> Use the strategic-document-builder skill to turn these customer interviews, market findings, and roadmap proposals into an executive-ready product strategy document. Clearly distinguish validated evidence from assumptions.

## Privacy
Review strategy inputs for customer-confidential information, internal company plans, and sensitive commercial data before sharing them with an AI tool or publishing outputs.

## Format
Uses the `SKILL.md` convention from [Agent Skills](https://agentskills.io/).
